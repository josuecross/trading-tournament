# Evidence Requirements

Future activation/output must produce a compact latest folder with no more than 10 files.

Required evidence:

- observation summary
- status CSV
- checkpoint CSV
- risk status
- signal snapshot
- benchmark comparison
- assumptions and warnings
- manifest
- advisor upload integration

Raw OHLCV must not be included in compact evidence or advisor upload. Broker, live order, order placement, account credential, and real-money recommendation data must not appear.
