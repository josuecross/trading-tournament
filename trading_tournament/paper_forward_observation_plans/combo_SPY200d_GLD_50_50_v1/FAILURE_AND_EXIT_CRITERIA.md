# Failure And Exit Criteria

## Failure / Review Triggers

- project stop hit
- drawdown worse than SPY_200d by a material threshold
- combo underperforms SPY_200d while taking more drawdown
- target probability thesis contradicted by paper observation
- data or signal missing
- rule hash mismatch
- evidence packet inconsistency
- observation drift from frozen rule
- manual intervention
- any broker, live order, order placement, or real-money behavior appears

## Interpretation

Failure does not mean real-money loss because this is simulated paper/demo evidence only.

## Exit Boundary

Stopping or failing an observation does not delete historical evidence and does not imply replacement of the frozen SPY_200d stream.
