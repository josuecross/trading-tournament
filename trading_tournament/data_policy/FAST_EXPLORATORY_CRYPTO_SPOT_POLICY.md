# Fast Exploratory Crypto Spot Policy

This policy applies only to early Tier 2 exploratory crypto spot historical screens.

Crypto spot data can be used for Tier 2 exploratory historical screens if basic QA passes. Results are exploratory, non-final, not paper-forward, and not real-money evidence.

For Crypto Spot Tier 2 Risk-Control Batch 1, the allowed crypto universe is BTC/ETH only:

- BTC-USD
- ETH-USD

The batch may use public or cached daily spot data for first-pass research. If a cache is already sufficient, no download should occur. If cache is missing, a controlled public-data acquisition may request only BTC-USD and ETH-USD.

Forbidden mechanics:

- no leverage
- no margin
- no shorting
- no futures
- no perpetual swaps
- no options
- no exchange execution
- no broker integration
- no live orders
- no order placement
- no real-money recommendation

Strict exchange, cost, spread, custody, tax, 24/7 calendar, session-alignment, and data-provider review is deferred until a candidate shows promise. Candidate_exhaustive and paper-forward lanes remain strict and require separate approval.

Crypto evidence remains separate from ETF/fund core evidence. A promising crypto row may justify only a future review prompt, not paper-forward activation or real-money use.
