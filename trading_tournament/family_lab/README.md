# Family Lab

This folder defines the independent family challenge comparison for the compact challenge audit.

Each family row receives its own separate $3,000 simulated paper/demo account. Families do not share capital, and these rows are not portfolio mixes or allocation advice. Portfolio mixes live separately under `portfolio_lab/`.

Runnable family rows use only already-implemented or already-cached evidence paths. Blocked families are reported as blocked rows and are not backtested. No row here recommends real-money trading, connects to a broker, places orders, or changes frozen paper-forward strategy rules.

Crypto-containing family rows are optional and remain Tier 1 exploratory only. Individual stocks, options, futures, forex, intraday, volatility products, and event/news families remain blocked unless their gates pass.
