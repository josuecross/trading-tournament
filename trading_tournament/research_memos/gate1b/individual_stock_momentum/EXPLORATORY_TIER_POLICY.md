# Exploratory Tier Policy

## Tier 0: Research Map Only

Allowed conclusion: the lane is worth mapping or not.

Forbidden conclusion: any performance claim.

## Tier 1: Toy/Current-Ticker Exploratory Screen

Allowed conclusion: code path works, rough behavior only, runtime/cost pressure can be observed.

Forbidden conclusion: strategy is profitable, credible, or validated.

## Tier 2: Credible Prototype

Requires survivorship controls or delisted-name coverage.

Allowed conclusion: candidate may deserve deeper review.

Forbidden conclusion: paper-forward readiness.

## Tier 3: Candidate Validation

Requires survivorship-free universe, delisting treatment, realistic costs, benchmarks, and evidence-tier controls.

Allowed conclusion: candidate may enter stronger validation.

Forbidden conclusion: real-money recommendation.

## Tier 4: Paper/Demo Observation

Allowed only after separate promotion review. No real-money tier is targeted.
