# Evidence Tier Policy

This policy prevents exploratory or memo-only work from being treated as validated strategy evidence.

## Tier 0: Research Map / Memo Only

Allowed conclusions: plausible, implausible, blocked, needs review.

Forbidden conclusions: validated, candidate, paper-forward ready, real-money suitable.

Required evidence: written memo, data/execution risks, gate status, blocked items.

Promotion criteria: Gate 0 and Gate 1 approval where applicable.

## Tier 1: Exploratory Non-Final

Allowed conclusions: interesting exploratory hypothesis, not worth further research, needs better data.

Forbidden conclusions: validated, reliable, paper-forward ready, real-money suitable.

Required evidence: isolated exploratory packet, clear limitations, no mixing with candidate evidence.

Promotion criteria: credible data, fixed execution assumptions, benchmark plan, and Tier 2 review.

## Tier 2: Credible Prototype

Allowed conclusions: credible prototype, needs candidate validation, still non-final.

Forbidden conclusions: real-money ready, validated, reliable income.

Required evidence: isolated implementation, data quality report, fixed rules, benchmark comparison, cost assumptions.

Promotion criteria: stress tests, rolling target-before-stop evidence, drawdown analysis, and anti-overfitting log.

## Tier 3: Candidate Validation

Allowed conclusions: practical candidate, watchlist, benchmark candidate, reject.

Forbidden conclusions: proven, guaranteed, real-money suitable.

Required evidence: full-period test, stress costs, exact or clearly labeled rolling windows, target-before-stop, benchmark comparison, stop audit.

Promotion criteria: fixed rules, no mid-test changes, documented candidate review.

## Tier 4: Paper-Forward Observation

Allowed conclusions: active observation, stopped, target reached, still under observation.

Forbidden conclusions: real-money recommendation, live-trading signal, validation by one outcome.

Required evidence: frozen rules, start date, daily log, risk status, benchmark comparison, no live orders.

Promotion criteria: none inside this project without a separate governance decision.

## Benchmark

Benchmarks are comparisons, not strategy recommendations.

## Blocked

Blocked rows cannot be implemented until the named gate or data/execution blocker is resolved.
