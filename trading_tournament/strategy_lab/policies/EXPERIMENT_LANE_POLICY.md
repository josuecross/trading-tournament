# Experiment Lane Policy

Parallel experiments are allowed only when isolated from frozen paper-forward rows.

Every experiment must:

- have a unique id and version,
- declare a lane,
- declare an evidence tier,
- declare allowed and forbidden next actions,
- write evidence separately,
- avoid modifying paper-forward rules,
- avoid claiming validation before promotion.

Exploratory results are non-final. Memo-only and blocked rows cannot become implementations without passing their gates.
