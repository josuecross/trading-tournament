# Profit Exploration League

This folder defines a research-only paper/demo profit exploration layer.

The league gives every experiment its own independent simulated $3,000 account and compares stop-aware profit potential beyond the +$300 and +$400 challenge hurdles. It does not replace the frozen paper-forward observation, does not change existing strategy rules, and does not recommend real-money trading.

Experiments are predeclared in `profit_experiment_specs.yaml`. Fixed combinations are independent experiment accounts, not allocation advice. Blocked families are reported but not run. Crypto rows, when included, remain Tier 1 exploratory only.

No broker integration, live orders, exchange trading, leverage, margin, shorting, hidden optimization, or grid search is allowed in this layer.
