# Fast Exploration Batch 1 Review

This batch broadens the fast exploratory universe beyond standalone commodity and crypto sleeves. The goal is to test whether a simple global ETF/fund-wrapper momentum universe can improve +$300/+400 target probability while staying closer to the -$600 drawdown budget.

The approved research universe for the primary sleeve is SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, EEM, IEF, TLT, GLD, PDBC, and COMT, with BIL as fallback. DBC, GSG, and USCI remain acquisition/commodity references only for this batch and are not part of the global multi-asset rule.

The evidence tier is tier1_or_tier2_exploratory. Public adjusted ETF/fund wrapper data is acceptable for this first fast screen if basic QA passes. Product, wrapper, tax, tracking, liquidity, and methodology risks remain non-final and must be reviewed later if any row appears promising.

This review does not recommend paper-forward promotion or real-money trading.
