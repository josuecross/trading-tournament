# Failure Criteria

A crypto Tier 2 risk-control candidate should remain watchlist, too-risky, too-slow, incomplete, or rejected if:

- 90d or 180d worst drawdown materially breaches the -$600 budget.
- Stop-hit rates are materially worse than combo/top2.
- +300/+400 target rates are diluted to trivial levels.
- Stress degradation is unacceptable.
- The row does not improve stop-aware score versus at least one primary benchmark.
- The combo-plus-crypto row inherits target windows from combo without incremental value.
- The result depends on unreviewed exchange/cost/24-7 assumptions.
- Crypto warnings are not visible.

Candidate_exhaustive, if ever recommended, is a future review only and cannot be run by this batch.
