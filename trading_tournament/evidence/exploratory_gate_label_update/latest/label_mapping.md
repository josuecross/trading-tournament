# Exploratory Gate Label Mapping

- `diversifier_watchlist_candidate`: low-correlation, acceptable-risk row that may be useful as a diversifier but is not promotion-ready.
- `short_history_watchlist`: wrapper/history is too short for strong conclusions.
- `benchmark_watchlist`: useful as a sanity-check or benchmark lane, not promotion-ready.
- `defensive_watchlist`: good drawdown/control profile but weak profit objective fit.
- `too_slow_for_profit_goal`: controlled risk but weak +300/+400 or median-profit behavior.
- `duplicate_watchlist`: duplicate-like comparison row worth retaining as a reference.
- `needs_benchmark_delta_review`: key benchmark comparison is missing, so no confident label upgrade is made.

All labels above are non-promotion labels and route to continued research/watching only.
