# Warnings And Limitations

- This is a candidate queue only.
- No strategy is implemented by this task.
- No backtest is run by this task.
- No candidate is validated by appearing in the queue.
- No candidate is paper-forward active.
- No paper-forward rule is changed.
- No real-money recommendation is made.
- No broker integration, live orders, or order placement are added.
- Candidate priority is provisional and must be revisited after current finalist validation.
- Data-gated candidates must not be coded before data gates pass.
- Execution-gated candidates must not be coded before execution realism gates pass.
- High-complexity instruments remain blocked or deferred.
- Individual stock momentum remains blocked until survivorship-free data and delisting treatment requirements pass.
- Crypto spot remains exploratory unless a Tier 2 data/execution memo is approved.
- Crypto leverage, perps, futures, margin, and shorting remain rejected.
- The queue is intended to prevent random strategy shopping, not to expand implementation scope automatically.
