# Diversification Audit

## Correlation Evidence Boundary

Correlation diagnostics are not available in the current Profit Exploration outputs beyond placeholder fields for some rows. This packet does not infer correlations from allocation labels alone. Any future diversification claim needs explicit daily return correlation, rolling stress correlation, drawdown co-incidence, and target-window co-movement.

## Which Candidates Add Real Diversification?

Managed-futures proxy is the most plausible diversifier by concept, because DBMF/KMLM are fund-wrapper proxies for managed-futures style exposure rather than U.S. equity sector or factor rotation. The actual research_sample row is still too slow and short-history-labeled, so it is not candidate_exhaustive material.

Combo SPY200d/GLD adds practical diversification relative to pure SPY exposure and remains the best current stop-aware balance.

## Which Mostly Add Equity Beta?

QQQ dual momentum mostly adds higher-beta equity upside during favorable windows. It improved target rates but materially worsened stop and drawdown risk.

Value/momentum factor ETF rotation mostly duplicated U.S. equity beta through factor ETF wrappers.

Sector top2 mostly represented sector equity beta; sector dispersion did not overcome drawdown/stop concerns.

## Which Reduce Drawdown But Are Too Slow?

Managed-futures proxy reduced drawdown versus many risky candidates but had weak target rates and heavy BIL allocation.

BIL is the pure defensive benchmark and is too slow by design.

## Which Improve Target Rates Only By Worsening Drawdown?

QQQ dual momentum and GLD buy-hold are the clearest examples. Both showed stronger target ladders, but their stop/drawdown behavior prevents advancement.

## Which Might Be Useful Later As Components?

Managed-futures proxy may be worth a later combination/diversification review, but only with short-history and fund-wrapper proxy warnings.

Sector top2 may remain a diagnostic component for sector dispersion, but not as a promoted row.

QQQ and value/momentum should remain references rather than components unless a new gate proves they add risk-adjusted diversification instead of equity beta.

## Which Should Not Be Combined Yet?

Do not combine recent failed or marginal candidates now. Combining QQQ, value/momentum, sector top2, or managed-futures proxy immediately after weak standalone evidence would be strategy shopping unless a new combination-design gate defines a fixed rule, failure criteria, and correlation tests first.

## Managed-Futures Proxy

Managed-futures proxy may add useful risk-control information, but the tested rule is too slow and based on short-history ETF/fund wrappers. It should stay watchlist only.

## Sector Top2

Sector top2 did not demonstrate enough beyond equity beta. It remains watchlist only because sector dispersion is plausible but unproven.

## Value/Momentum Factor Rotation

Value/momentum factor rotation did not add enough beyond equity beta and should be archived as a duplicate reference.

## QQQ

QQQ mostly added higher beta and stop/drawdown risk. It should be held only as a high-upside/high-risk reference.
