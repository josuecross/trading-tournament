# Research Priority Decision

Decision: `choose_commodity_basket_etf_momentum_review`

## Why Commodity Basket ETF Momentum

Commodity basket ETF momentum is the best next historical family to move into a product/data review because it:

- can be gated through ETF/fund product review rather than blocked stock-provider access,
- may add commodity/inflation/roll-return exposure beyond equity-beta variants,
- is less obviously a direct duplicate of QQQ/value/sector momentum than more equity ETF variants,
- can use a conservative review-first workflow before any data download,
- can explicitly address product structure, ETN/ETF differences, roll yield, inception history, and tax/product risks.

## Why Not Continue Stock Provider Loops Now

Individual stock momentum remains strategically interesting, but Norgate is blocked by no local access and Sharadar is conditional on package/terms selection. More stock-provider work now would require user provider selection or access confirmation rather than quantitative research work.

This is better than continuing stock-provider review loops right now because the blocker is access/terms selection, not a research-ranking question that can be resolved from existing local evidence.

## Why Not Treasury First

Treasury duration is accessible and useful for defense, but target potential for +300/+400 hurdles is likely weaker. It remains second choice.

## Why Not Crypto, Volatility, Macro, Or More Factor/Sector

Crypto remains Tier 2 and separate. Volatility proxies carry wrapper/futures-roll risks. Macro filters risk becoming tuning/filter shopping. More factor/sector work risks repeating equity-beta duplicates.

## Decision Boundary

This decision approves only a future commodity basket ETF product/data review prompt. It does not approve implementation, data download, backtest, Profit Exploration, candidate_exhaustive, paper-forward observation, or real-money recommendation.
