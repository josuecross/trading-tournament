# Gate 1D Decision

Decision: `choose_norgate_for_gate1e_acquisition_review`

## Meaning

Norgate Data should be the first provider path for a future Gate 1E controlled acquisition review, assuming the user accepts cost/access requirements and the EULA/cache-rights review does not reveal blockers.

This is not a data-download approval. It is not stock strategy implementation approval. It is not backtest approval. It is not Profit Exploration approval. It is not paper-forward approval.

## Preferred Provider

Preferred provider: `Norgate Data`

Reason: Norgate is the most practical serious path reviewed for an individual research workflow because public documentation indicates survivorship-bias-free market data, delisted securities at appropriate subscription levels, historical constituent support through integrations, and local-machine data access.

## Secondary Provider

Secondary provider: `Nasdaq Data Link / Sharadar`

Reason: Sharadar may be a serious API-style path if active/delisted coverage, delisting treatment, PIT/all-listed universe support, and cache rights are verified.

## Academic Reference

Reason: CRSP appears strongest for academic-grade delisting-return treatment and market history, but access is likely institutional or restricted.
