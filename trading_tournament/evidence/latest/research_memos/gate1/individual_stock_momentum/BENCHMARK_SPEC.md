# Benchmark Spec

Individual stock momentum must be compared against benchmarks that expose whether it adds value beyond beta, ETF momentum, and simple cash alternatives.

## Required Benchmarks

- SPY buy-and-hold: broad equity beta baseline.
- Equal-weight broad stock universe: tests whether the stock system beats simple equal-weight stock exposure.
- Market-cap-weighted broad stock benchmark if available: tests against investable market-like exposure.
- Stock momentum benchmark if available: compares against an external or simple internal momentum baseline.
- Current ETF momentum candidate: checks whether stock complexity improves on ETF Phase 1.
- Cash/BIL proxy: no-risk or low-risk opportunity cost baseline.
- 60/40 proxy: balanced portfolio benchmark.
- No-trade baseline: confirms that activity adds value over doing nothing.

## Benchmark Requirement

If the broad stock universe benchmark cannot be built without survivorship bias, serious Gate 2 approval should remain blocked.
