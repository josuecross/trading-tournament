# Cost And Access Notes

This review did not verify contracts, exact pricing, or trial availability. Cost categories below are feasibility labels, not price quotes.

## CRSP

- Likely access burden: institutional.
- Public pricing visible in this review: not verified.
- Academic or commercial access may be required: likely.
- API/export availability: not verified for this project.
- Practicality for a solo local project: uncertain.
- Cost/access blocker: yes, until access is confirmed.

## Norgate Data

- Likely access burden: moderate.
- Public pricing visible in this review: not verified in this packet.
- Subscription likely needed: yes.
- API/export availability: publicly described, including Python.
- Practicality for a solo local project: possible.
- Cost/access blocker: still unresolved.

## Nasdaq Data Link / Sharadar

- Likely access burden: moderate to high.
- Public pricing visible in this review: not verified.
- Subscription likely needed: yes.
- API/export availability: likely through Nasdaq Data Link, but package specifics need review.
- Practicality for a solo local project: possible only if required datasets and license are affordable.
- Cost/access blocker: unresolved.

## Polygon

- Likely access burden: moderate to high depending plan.
- API/export availability: yes for market-data APIs.
- Serious research practicality: unresolved because survivorship/delisting is not verified.
- Cost/access blocker: unresolved.

## Tiingo

- Likely access burden: moderate.
- API/export availability: yes.
- Serious research practicality: unresolved.
- Cost/access blocker: unresolved.

## EODHD

- Likely access burden: low to moderate.
- API/export availability: yes.
- Serious research practicality: unresolved.
- Cost/access blocker: unresolved.

## Interactive Brokers

- Likely access burden: account/platform dependent.
- API/export availability: yes.
- Serious research practicality: poor as a primary research database.
- Cost/access blocker: not the main blocker; data suitability is.

## Alpaca

- Likely access burden: low to moderate.
- API/export availability: yes.
- Serious research practicality: poor as a primary research database.
- Cost/access blocker: secondary to data suitability.

## yfinance / Current Tickers

- Likely access burden: low.
- API/export availability: Python package download wrapper.
- Serious research practicality: unsuitable for validation.
- Cost/access blocker: no, but data validity is the blocker.

## Cost/Access Conclusion

CRSP and Norgate are the most credible follow-up paths. CRSP may be too institutional for the project. Norgate may be more practical, but cost, license, delisting-return treatment, and local caching rights must be verified before any Gate 2 decision.
