# License And Usage Risks

This packet does not grant permission to ingest, cache, redistribute, or publish vendor data. Every vendor license must be reviewed before any data is downloaded or stored.

## Main License Risks

- Data redistribution restrictions may prevent sharing raw vendor rows in evidence packets.
- Local caching may be restricted or governed by subscription terms.
- Derived research outputs may be allowed while raw data redistribution is prohibited; this must be verified.
- Screenshots or sample data rows may be restricted.
- API terms may limit retention, redistribution, or commercial use.
- Personal-use or educational-use language may not permit broader project sharing.
- Commercial and academic licenses can differ materially.
- Vendor data committed to git could violate license terms.
- Evidence packets shared externally could accidentally contain proprietary raw data.

## Required Project Rule

No proprietary vendor data should be committed to git or uploaded in evidence packets. Evidence packets should contain summary metrics and audit metadata, not raw licensed price histories.

## Gate 2 License Requirements

Before any serious isolated prototype:

- Confirm local cache rights.
- Confirm whether derived metrics and audit summaries can be shared.
- Confirm whether raw data samples are prohibited in evidence packets.
- Confirm whether access is personal, academic, commercial, or otherwise restricted.
- Confirm whether the project can keep reproducible run metadata without exposing licensed records.
- Confirm whether vendor terms allow automated local research runs.

## Current License Conclusion

Gate 2 remains blocked because license and usage terms have not been verified for any serious data candidate.
