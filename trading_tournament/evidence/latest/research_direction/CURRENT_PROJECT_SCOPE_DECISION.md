# Current Project Scope Decision

## 1. Should ETF Phase 1 Continue?

Yes. ETF Phase 1 should continue because the current framework is reproducible, auditable, and suitable for learning which fixed-rule daily ETF strategies are plausible.

## 2. Should ETFs Be Treated As The Entire Answer?

No. ETFs are Phase 1, not the whole investigation. The broader question includes other instruments and strategy families, but those require feasibility review before implementation.

## 3. Should High-Risk Instruments Be Added Now?

No. Options, futures, forex, crypto leverage, volatility products, intraday strategies, and event/news strategies are not ready for implementation because the project lacks the required data and execution models.

## 4. Should Strategy Expansion Continue?

Only after scope control. Expansion should proceed through the opportunity map and gate policy, not through random coding.

## 5. Should Current A/B/C/D/E Remain Active?

A and B can remain historical comparators. C, D, and E should not remain active forward candidates based on current loss-budget evidence.

## 6. Should C/D/E Be Archived?

Yes. C/D/E should be archived from active comparison and treated as shadow-only or rejected unless separately revalidated with fixed rules and no tuning.

## 7. Should Evidence-Backed ETF Strategies Continue?

Yes. Evidence-backed ETF families such as dual momentum, tactical asset allocation, volatility scaling, and defensive allocation deserve controlled ETF Phase 1/2 research.

## 8. Should AI Be Added?

No. AI gating, market scoring, position sizing, and trade permission should not be added. AI may later help summarize evidence packets or write audit questions, but it should not influence trades.

## 9. Current Status

The project is between Phase 1 and Phase 2. It has a useful ETF research lab and evidence workflow, but it needs scope control before adding non-ETF instruments.

## Recommended Conclusion

- Continue ETF Phase 1.
- Do not treat ETFs as the whole investigation.
- Do not implement high-risk instruments yet.
- Do not add AI gating.
- Freeze active ETF candidates to a small set.
- Archive C/D/E from active comparison.
- Create and follow the broad opportunity map before expanding implementation.

No real-money recommendation.
