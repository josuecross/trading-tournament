# Equity Beta Risk Review

## Equity Concentration

QQQ increases growth-equity concentration. If QQQ dominates selection, the strategy may become a higher-beta equity momentum variant rather than a diversified asset-class rotation.

## Target Improvement Risk

Any improvement in +300/+400/+600 target rates could come mainly from higher beta. That is not sufficient. The strategy must also control:

- stop-hit rate
- worst drawdown
- drawdown-budget usage
- stress degradation
- median stop-enforced equity
- p95 stop-enforced equity

## Metrics Required In Research Sample

A later research_sample implementation must report:

- 30/60/90/180 target ladder
- stop-hit rate
- worst drawdown
- risk-budget usage against -$600
- stress degradation
- allocation concentration by asset
- QQQ selection frequency
- comparison to SPY/top2/combo

## Risk Conclusion

The candidate is allowed for future research_sample only if the review remains explicit that QQQ may increase equity-beta risk. Higher target probability alone cannot justify advancement.
