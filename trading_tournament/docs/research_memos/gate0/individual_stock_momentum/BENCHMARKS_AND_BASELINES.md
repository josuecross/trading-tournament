# Benchmarks And Baselines

Individual stock momentum is only useful if it beats ETF alternatives or improves target-before-stop probability without unacceptable drawdown or execution assumptions.

## Required Benchmarks

- SPY buy-and-hold.
- Equal-weight current ETF basket.
- 60/40 proxy.
- Cash/BIL proxy.
- SPY 200-day trend model.
- Broad stock universe equal-weight benchmark.
- Large-cap momentum benchmark if available.
- Simple ETF momentum baseline.
- Current best ETF candidate.
- No-trade/cash baseline.

## Why These Matter

SPY and ETF benchmarks show whether the stock system is adding value beyond equity beta. A broad stock universe benchmark shows whether any return is simply from equal-weight stock exposure. A large-cap momentum benchmark helps avoid comparing a complex system to an irrelevant baseline.

The current best ETF candidate is especially important. If stock momentum adds data complexity, gap risk, and execution uncertainty without improving rolling target-before-stop rates, it should not move forward.
