# Stop / Defer / Keep List

## KEEP

- ETF Phase 1.
- `evidence/latest` workflow.
- Adjusted OHLC handling.
- Stress slippage.
- Benchmark comparison.
- Target-before-stop analysis.
- Validation modes.
- Candidate gating.
- Anti-overfitting log.
- Research-only boundary.

## STOP

- Adding new code before the opportunity map is used.
- Treating ETF-only results as the full answer.
- Active C/D/E comparisons as forward candidates.
- Exhaustive validation during normal development.
- Interpreting one target hit as proof.
- AI trading or gating.
- High-risk instrument implementation.
- Repeated strategy redesign to chase profit.
- Treating engineering sophistication as evidence of edge.

## DEFER

- Options.
- Futures.
- Forex.
- Crypto.
- Intraday.
- Event/news.
- Volatility products.
- AI report auditor.
- Broker-like simulator.
- Live paper broker connection.

Deferred does not mean impossible. It means not now, not without a memo, and not without an execution/data model.
