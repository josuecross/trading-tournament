# Research Direction Summary

## 1. Executive Decision

Continue ETF Phase 1 and evidence-backed ETF research, but do not implement high-risk or non-ETF instruments yet. First complete the opportunity map and gate policy. The project remains valuable as research/learning/portfolio work even if no strategy reaches the target reliably.

## 2. Correct Interpretation Of Goal

The +$300 to +$400 objective is a challenge metric, not proof of income potential. The important evidence is rolling target-before-stop probability, time-to-target, drawdown, and stress-cost survival.

## 3. What To Keep

Keep adjusted OHLC, ETF Phase 1, evidence packets, stress slippage, benchmark comparison, validation modes, candidate gating, and the anti-overfitting log.

## 4. What To Stop

Stop treating one target hit as proof. Stop active C/D/E comparisons as forward candidates. Stop adding code before scope documents and gate decisions.

## 5. What To Defer

Defer options, futures, forex, crypto, volatility products, intraday trading, event/news strategies, AI trade gating, and broker-like simulation.

## 6. Current Implementation Boundary

The current implementation boundary is daily ETF research. Evidence-backed ETF families may continue, but the active candidate set should remain small.

## 7. Opportunity Map Summary

The opportunity map shows that many instruments can reach the target in theory, but most are disqualified for now by execution realism, leverage, data quality, or modeling complexity.

## 8. Instrument Classes Not Ready For Implementation

Options, futures, forex, crypto leverage, volatility products, intraday strategies, and event/news strategies are not ready for code.

## 9. What Evidence Would Justify Expansion

Expansion requires clean data, realistic execution assumptions, benchmark definition, risk model, failure criteria, and an isolated prototype plan.

## 10. Single Next Research Step

Use `INSTRUMENT_AND_STRATEGY_MATRIX.csv` to decide which non-ETF areas deserve Gate 0 research memos, not prototypes.

Conclusion: continue ETF Phase 1 and evidence-backed ETF research, but do not implement high-risk or non-ETF instruments yet. First complete the opportunity map and gate policy. The project remains valuable as research/learning/portfolio work even if no strategy reaches the target reliably.
